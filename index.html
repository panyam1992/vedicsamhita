            Day & Night Choghadiya Timings

            Principles of Choghadiya:
            • Amrit (Chandra): Highly Auspicious — Best for all good beginnings, celebrations & pujas.
            • Shubh (Guru): Auspicious — Best for ceremonies, education, signing contracts & official work.
            • Labh (Budha): Auspicious — Best for commerce, financial deals, buying vehicles & investments.
            • Char (Shukra): Auspicious — Best for travel, journeys, moving & dynamic activities.
            • Udveg (Surya): Inauspicious — Anxiety & obstacles; avoid new ventures.
            • Rog (Kuja): Inauspicious — Conflicts & illness; avoid medical starts or disputes.
            • Kaal (Shani): Inauspicious — Ruled by Shani; strictly avoid starting any auspicious venture.
